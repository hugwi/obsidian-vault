How the AI Editing Workflow Works

First, you upload your raw clip through the AutoEdit Premiere Pro extension. You then enter a short prompt explaining what the video is about.

The plugin uploads the recording for cloud processing, transcribes the dialogue, analyzes the context, and suggests which sections should be removed or kept.

Before anything is applied to your Premiere Pro timeline, you can review the suggested edits, check the good and bad takes, and adjust the amount of silence left around each sentence.

Once the rough cut is applied, you can continue editing inside Premiere Pro by adding captions, B-roll, music, graphics, sound effects, and any final creative decisions.

AutoEdit helps build the starting structure. The editor still reviews and finishes the video.

What is AutoEdit Creator Mode?

AutoEdit Creator Mode is an AI-assisted Adobe Premiere Pro plugin that helps create a rough cut by identifying repeated takes, filler words, and long pauses. You review the suggested edits before continuing your edit.

Does AutoEdit replace the editor?

No. AutoEdit creates a suggested rough cut to reduce repetitive editing. You still review the timeline and finish the creative edit inside Premiere Pro.

What videos does it work best for?

Creator Mode works best for YouTube videos, podcasts, talking-head content, tutorials, explainers, interviews, voice-overs, and other dialogue-heavy videos.

Does it work inside Premiere Pro?

Yes. AutoEdit runs as an Adobe Premiere Pro extension, so you can review and apply edits without leaving Premiere.
