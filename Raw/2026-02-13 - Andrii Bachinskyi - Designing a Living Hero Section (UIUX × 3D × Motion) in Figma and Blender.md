# Designing a Living Hero Section (UI/UX × 3D × Motion) in Figma and Blender

![Designing a Living Hero Section (UI/UX × 3D × Motion) in Figma and Blender](https://www.youtube.com/embed/q1QQN08ZK6I)

## Description

Catch this tutorial for my new concept - the first screen website design for Smart Sphere Club ⚪️🧠

In this Reel, I walk through the process of building a hero section from scratch - starting with clean UI and evolving it into a meaningful 3D scene. The concept is built for a club and forum for technological reflection, ideas, and future-focused dialogue.

🧩 Figma - grid system, typography, content hierarchy, and UI/UX logic
🌀 Blender - working with spheres, cuts, volume, materials, and visual metaphors
🎞 After Effects - animation, pacing, transitions, and final presentation

This is about combining UI/UX design, 3D, and motion to create a first screen that feels alive, intentional, and conceptual - not just decorative.
