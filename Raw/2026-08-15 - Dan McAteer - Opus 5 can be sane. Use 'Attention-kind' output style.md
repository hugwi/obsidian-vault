Human-Computer Interaction is a flourishing field of study.

But computers can now interact with *us*. 

Humans interacting with AI is now a two-way street, and we need a field of study to understand the best way to design that interaction.

Think about it like a reverse-harness. One designed not for how humans best interact with AI, but for how AI best interacts with humans.
