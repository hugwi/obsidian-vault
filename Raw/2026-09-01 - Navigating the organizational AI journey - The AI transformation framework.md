# Navigating the organizational AI journey: The AI transformation framework

## TL;DR

A 2026 paper in Business Horizons that introduces the "AI transformation framework": a cube model of three dimensions — automation, augmentation, and data richness — plus a three-step process (path framing, path narrating, path stretching) to guide organizations through AI adoption. The key takeaway is that transformation should be incremental and grounded in where the organization actually sits across those three axes, not aimed straight at full integration.

## Key points

- Organizations can score themselves on three dimensions (0–16 each): **automation** (delegating tasks to AI), **augmentation** (AI enhancing human decision-making), and **data richness** (quality/accessibility of organizational data).
- There are eight quadrants in the cube model, from "manual isolated data scarcity" (low/low/low) to "integrated intelligence" (high/high/high) — the aspirational sweet spot, but not necessarily the right short-term target.
- **Path framing** defines the "what" (AI strategy/vision), **path narrating** defines the "when" (sequencing decisions over time), **path stretching** defines the "how" (scaling through partnerships, upskilling, resource allocation).
- The framework argues against big-bang AI transformation: instead, organizations should assess their current quadrant, then take a sequential, patient approach to improve each dimension.
- Three value-creation levers: pool data resources across orgs, automate low-hanging fruit, and augment key processes by learning from successful examples.
- The paper includes a 12-question self-assessment questionnaire (4 items per dimension, scored 0–4, total 0–16 per dimension).
