## Transcript

Clod invited me to compete in a special hackathon in New York City and I won first place. So, here's how I did it. The day started off in the Asian Art Department in the Met where competitors had to find a way to make art more immersive for museum visitors. My idea was to allow users to upload photos to a website and then turn the instruments in the painting into virtual playable versions. So, then I got to work. Using Clod code and help from my friend Steven, we created this insane web app. So, here's how it works. First, take a photo of your instrument, then the app uses AI and a custom sound engine to recreate the instrument. And finally, it was presentation time. I was actually really nervous because some of these projects were insane. Then it was my turn to present my project. I was actually really nervous on stage a lot. And when everyone was done, we all waited for the award ceremony. Number one was the playable instrument. I cannot believe I won a coding hackathon with zero coding experience. Thank you, Clod and the Met for making this possible and follow to join me on my next adventure. Peace.
