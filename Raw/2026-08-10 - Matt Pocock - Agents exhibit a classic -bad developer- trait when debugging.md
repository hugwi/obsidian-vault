# Agents exhibit a classic "bad developer" trait when debugging

> Saved from X on 2026-08-10. Author: Matt Pocock.

Agents exhibit a classic "bad developer" trait when debugging

They start to get hopeful. They say "we're getting closer". "This is the decisive piece of information".

This narrative builds until they say something like "we've eliminated all possible hypotheses". Or "this is the definitive fix".

Pull back. Don't jump to conclusions. Soft eyes.

Just had this happen - it ruled out all possible hypotheses, and said it was an upstream bug.

I said "don't jump to conclusions yet". 2 turns later, the bug was solved.
