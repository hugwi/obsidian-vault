# Nvidia Unveils Groq 3 LPU for Vera Rubin AI Inference

![Nvidia Unveils Groq 3 LPU for Vera Rubin AI Inference](https://www.youtube.com/embed/FwBlWOX2rHQ)

## Description

Nvidia has introduced Groq 3 LPU support for its Vera Rubin AI platform, aiming to make inference faster, lower-latency and better suited for the next generation of agentic AI systems.

In this Xenon Lab News episode, we look at the Groq 3 LPU, a specialized language processing unit designed not for training, but for real-time AI inference. Each accelerator includes 500MB of SRAM with an enormous 150TB/s of memory bandwidth. That is a very different approach from traditional AI GPUs, which rely heavily on large HBM memory pools.

The key idea is speed. SRAM capacity is much smaller than HBM, but its bandwidth and latency can be extremely useful when AI models need to generate tokens quickly, coordinate agents and process long-context workloads.

Nvidia is also scaling this technology into Groq 3 LPX racks. A full rack includes 256 LPU accelerators, creating a high-bandwidth inference system that works alongside Vera Rubin GPUs. Nvidia sees LPX as a co-processor that can boost decoding performance across every layer of an AI model and every generated token.

This could become an important shift in AI infrastructure. Training made Nvidia the leader of the AI boom, but inference may become the next major battlefield.
