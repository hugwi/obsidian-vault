# What's stopping us from regrowing limbs? - Michael Levin #biology #medicine

![What's stopping us from regrowing limbs? - Michael Levin #biology #medicine](https://www.youtube.com/embed/GIIxFCOCV6o)

## Description

Limb regeneration is common in animals like axolotls and other amphibians, but it doesn't occur in mammals like mice and humans. Michael Levin (Tufts University) explains his early experiments in creating biodomes, which are wearable bioreactors that stimulate limb growth.

The biodomes contain a gel with medicines inside to promote regrowth. Surprisingly, even the negative control, without any medicine, stimulated some level of regeneration in adult frogs.

Do you think this medical technology can ever develop into something helpful for healing wounds and treating amputees?
