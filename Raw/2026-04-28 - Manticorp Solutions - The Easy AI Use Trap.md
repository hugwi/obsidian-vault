# The Easy AI Use Trap

![The Easy AI Use Trap](https://www.youtube.com/embed/1Q66n32s_iQ)

## Description

Unlike Photoshop or Slack, AI tools have no clear ceiling, so engineers default to the easiest use and never push past it. That's why most rollouts quietly stall.

## Transcript

Your AI pilot doesn't need an AI expert. It needs two weeks and one person doing this. When I look at how engineering teams actually use these tools from the inside, what I see is that each engineer has figured out some way that the tool works for them and then never push past that. One uses it for boilerplate, one for test cases, one for quick questions. They've defaulted to the easiest use case, not the best one. Tools like Photoshop or Slack have a clear ceiling. You know what you're trying to do and when you've done it. AI isn't like that. Its capability is vast and unless someone is actively pushing your team to reach the limits of it, they'll just stick with what they know. Just buying AI seats isn't enough. You need to have someone pushing your team past the basic stuff.
