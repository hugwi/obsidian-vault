# Why I'll never raise or hire:

> Saved from X on 2026-08-12. Author: Marc Lou.

Why I'll never raise or hire:

I wake up not knowing what I’ll do today.
I have breakfast with my wife while my phone is off.
I hit the gym.
I do 4 hours of deep work on whatever sparks my creativity.
I have lunch with my wife when I’m hungry.
I answer support. Every customer is super kind.
I get paid for my work.
I close my laptop whenever I want.
I go to bed excited to wake up and do it all again.
