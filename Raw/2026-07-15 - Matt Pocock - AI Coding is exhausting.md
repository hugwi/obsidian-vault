## Description

Exploring the mental shift that AI has brought to coding. In this video, we discuss how AI has eliminated the tactical, routine aspects of programming, pushing developers to operate at a higher strategic level. This means less time on grunt work, but significantly more mental strain from constant high-level decision-making.
