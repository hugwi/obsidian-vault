However, once you look under the shroud, you are actually looking at the exact same core architecture as the standard sixteen gigabyte RX 9070 XT. That extra memory buffer is designed specifically for local AI model processing and heavy workstation tasks rather than rendering game textures, meaning the extra capacity is built for compute power first and foremost.

While it will still game brilliantly and deliver the solid frame rates you would expect from an RX 9070 XT, that extra VRAM offers no tangible gaming performance boost over the cheaper sixteen gigabyte version. It is an impressive piece of kit if you run local LLMs or heavy creative workloads on the side, but purely for gaming, the standard card remains the smarter buy.
