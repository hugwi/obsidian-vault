# HSD17B13 splice variant and chronic liver disease

*Synopsis written for this corpus from the published findings; not a verbatim abstract.*

An exome-sequencing study in a large health-system cohort identified a splice
variant (rs72613567:TA) in HSD17B13, a hepatic lipid-droplet protein, associated
with reduced risk of chronic liver disease. Carriers showed lower odds of both
alcoholic and non-alcoholic cirrhosis, and reduced progression from simple
steatosis to steatohepatitis. The variant produces a truncated, functionally
impaired protein — a partial loss of function.

The protective association was independent of, and partly mitigated, the risk
conferred by the PNPLA3 I148M allele. Aminotransferase levels were lower in
carriers in a dose-dependent fashion.
