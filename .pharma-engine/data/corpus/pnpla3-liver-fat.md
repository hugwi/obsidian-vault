# PNPLA3 I148M and hepatic fat content

*Synopsis written for this corpus from the published findings; not a verbatim abstract.*

A genome-wide association study of hepatic fat content measured by proton
magnetic resonance spectroscopy identified a non-synonymous coding variant in
PNPLA3 (rs738409, I148M) strongly associated with increased hepatic fat. The
association was present across ancestries and accounted for a substantial part of
the population variation in liver fat.

Carriers also showed higher hepatic inflammation. The risk allele produces a
protein variant that accumulates on lipid droplets rather than being degraded,
and subsequent work established it as the strongest common genetic determinant of
the spectrum from steatosis through steatohepatitis to cirrhosis.
