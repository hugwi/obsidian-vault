# Selonsertib in bridging fibrosis and cirrhosis due to steatohepatitis

*Synopsis written for this corpus from the published findings; not a verbatim abstract.*

Two randomised, placebo-controlled Phase 3 trials evaluated selonsertib, an
inhibitor of apoptosis signal-regulating kinase 1 (ASK1, encoded by MAP3K5), in
patients with steatohepatitis and bridging fibrosis (STELLAR-3) or compensated
cirrhosis (STELLAR-4).

Neither trial met its primary endpoint. The proportion of patients achieving at
least a one-stage improvement in fibrosis without worsening of steatohepatitis
did not differ significantly from placebo at week 48 in either study. Both trials
were terminated early for lack of efficacy. ASK1 inhibition had been supported by
preclinical rodent models of hepatic fibrosis and by a positive Phase 2 signal
that did not replicate at scale.
