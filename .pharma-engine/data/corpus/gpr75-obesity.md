# GPR75 protein-truncating variants and protection from obesity

*Synopsis written for this corpus from the published findings; not a verbatim abstract.*

Exome sequencing of roughly 640,000 individuals identified protein-truncating
variants in GPR75, an orphan G-protein-coupled receptor, in approximately 1 in
3,000 people. Heterozygous carriers had lower BMI — on the order of 1.8 kg/m2 —
and around 54% lower odds of obesity. Mice heterozygous or homozygous for Gpr75
disruption were protected against diet-induced weight gain and showed improved
glycaemic control.

The direction is unambiguous: loss of GPR75 function is protective, so
antagonism is the therapeutic move.
