# Phenome-wide consequences of genetically proxied IL-6 receptor blockade

*Synopsis written for this corpus from the published findings; not a verbatim abstract.*

Using the IL6R Asp358Ala variant as an instrument for lifelong reduced IL-6
receptor signalling, phenome-wide Mendelian randomisation reproduced the known
protective effect on coronary artery disease and on rheumatoid arthritis.

The same analysis identified adverse associations consistent with the safety
profile observed clinically with IL-6 receptor blockade: increased risk of
atopic dermatitis, cellulitis, urinary tract infection and cholecystitis. The
authors argue that the on-target adverse-effect profile of an IL-6 pathway drug
is largely predictable from human genetics before a trial is run.
