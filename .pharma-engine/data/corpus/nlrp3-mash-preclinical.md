# NLRP3 inflammasome blockade in a dietary model of steatohepatitis

*Synopsis written for this corpus from the published findings; not a verbatim abstract.*

MCC950, a selective small-molecule inhibitor of the NLRP3 inflammasome, was
administered to mice fed an atherogenic diet that produces steatohepatitis with
fibrosis. Treated animals showed reduced hepatic expression of inflammatory
markers, fewer activated caspase-1-positive cells, and reduced fibrosis on
histology compared with vehicle.

The authors conclude that NLRP3 is a plausible therapeutic target in human
steatohepatitis. No human data are presented in this study.
