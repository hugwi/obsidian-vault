# INHBE loss of function and body fat distribution

*Synopsis written for this corpus from the published findings; not a verbatim abstract.*

Two independent exome-wide association analyses in UK Biobank reported that
predicted loss-of-function variants in INHBE, which encodes the liver-secreted
activin ligand activin E, associate with lower waist-to-hip ratio adjusted for
BMI. Carriers showed a metabolic profile consistent with more favourable fat
distribution, including lower triglycerides.

INHBE is expressed almost exclusively in the liver and is induced in states of
hepatic lipid excess. The direction of the human genetic signal is that less
activin E is metabolically favourable.
