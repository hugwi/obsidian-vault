**CkliQ |** Hotel Management System Dashboard UI Design

CkliQ is a cutting-edge Hotel Property Management System (PMS) designed to transform the chaotic complexity of hospitality operations into a streamlined, visual command center. By unifying critical workflows—from reservation scheduling and housekeeping coordination to guest CRM and revenue analytics—into a single intuitive platform, CkliQ empowers hotel staff to manage high-volume daily operations with precision. The application moves beyond legacy spreadsheet-style interfaces, offering a modern SaaS solution that prioritizes operational clarity, ensuring front-desk teams can process check-ins faster and managers can make data-driven decisions instantly.

Aesthetically, CkliQ is built on a modular "Bento Grid" design system, utilizing a clean card-based layout that significantly reduces cognitive load by compartmentalizing complex data into digestible widgets. The visual identity features a sophisticated Royal Purple primary palette for active states, balanced by a Vibrant Teal for success metrics, set against a high-brightness, minimalist white background. This operational design ethic focuses on "glanceability," using interactive Gantt charts and traffic-light status indicators to allow users to assess property health in milliseconds, creating a user experience that is as functional as it is beautiful.

The Main Dashboard serves as the "heartbeat" of the hotel, designed to provide an instant health check of the property without overwhelming the user. It features a high-level Occupancy Spline Chart for visualizing weekly booking trends and a dedicated "Arriving Today" feed that allows front-desk staff to prioritize incoming VIPs and pending check-ins.

To combat information overload, I utilized a Bento-style layout, grouping key metrics like "Revenue" and "Room Status" into floating cards with soft shadows. The operational color logic is front and center here: Royal Purple guides the eye to active data points, while Teal offers quick reassurance on available inventory, creating a "Visual Command Center" that is both powerful and easy to scan.

"Booking Timeline" screen reimagines the traditional reservation list as a fully interactive Gantt-style timeline, solving the complex problem of inventory management and overbooking. By mapping room numbers against a horizontal date axis, reservationists can visualize gaps in occupancy and drag-and-drop bookings to optimize floor usage in real-time.

The design utilizes "Smart Pills" to convey context instantly: Purple for confirmed bookings, Teal for active check-outs, and Red for maintenance blocks. This spatial visualization allows staff to manage logistics—like keeping noisy groups away from business travelers—purely through visual cues, turning a complex logistics task into a simple, intuitive interaction.

Bridging the gap between the digital Front Desk and the physical Housekeeping staff, this screen uses a modular card system to track the real-time condition of every unit. Unlike text-heavy legacy lists, these visual cards display critical context at a glance, including guest avatars for occupied rooms and "Estimated Cleaning Time" timers for dirty units.

The design ethic here focuses on rapid status recognition using a strict "Traffic Light" system: Teal badges clearly signal rooms ready for new guests, while Orange icons alert staff to pending cleaning tasks. This visual clarity reduces communication errors and ensures that the Front Desk never assigns a guest to an unready room.

Designed for General Managers and Owners, the Analytics screen turns chaotic data into strategic insights through high-fidelity data visualization. The layout is anchored by large, legible KPI cards tracking "Total Revenue" and "ADR Growth," ensuring the most critical financial health metrics are visible within seconds.

The visual highlight is the "Revenue Trends" area chart, which uses smooth purple and teal gradients to visualize Year-over-Year performance curves. Supported by stacked bar charts for "Revenue by Source," this screen balances heavy data density with ample whitespace, making complex financial reporting feel accessible, modern, and actionable.

The CkliQ Inbox unifies all guest communication into a streamlined, split-pane interface, ensuring that no request—from extra towels to late check-outs—slips through the cracks. The design prioritizes context, embedding "Room Number" tags and "VIP Status" directly into the chat header so staff don't have to switch screens to identify the guest.

Visually, the chat interface is clean and conversational, using the brand's signature Royal Purple for staff responses to maintain professional consistency. By integrating rich media support for invoices and photos, this screen transforms the concierge experience from a transactional task into a personalized, 5-star dialogue.
