## A financial dashboard built around one question: where is the money sitting right now?

Lintel is a B2B SaaS platform where distributors track every job from first quote to closed deal. Pipeline value, stage-level breakdown and revenue trend all read from a single screen before you drill into individual rows.

**Inside the shot:**

\- Pipeline value with weekly / monthly / quarterly toggle

\- Revenue split into won value vs. value entering the pipeline

\- Stage breakdown across New, Quoted, Ordered and Action Required

\- Active jobs table with status filters, search, export and pagination

\- KPI cards with inline sparklines for volume and quote velocity

The hard part was density. A distributor runs 50+ open deals at once, so the layout had to surface financial health at a glance without burying the row-level detail their team actually works in.

Blue carries the data, grey carries context, orange is reserved for the single primary action on the page.
